Your LLC restructures entirely around maximizing R&D tax credit capture. All other services become secondary or eliminated.

## PRIMARY REVENUE STREAM: AI Implementation Projects (100% focus)

**Project scope:** Building custom AI/ML solutions with documented technical uncertainty and systematic experimentation.

**Project types that qualify:**
- Custom ML model development (training, validation, hyperparameter optimization)
- Novel data pipeline architecture & feature engineering
- Algorithm optimization for performance/accuracy
- AI system integration with technical complexity (scalability, latency testing)

**Project pricing:** $50K–$500K per engagement
- Typical: $150K–$300K, 4–6 month duration
- Billed as implementation revenue (not strategy, not retainer)

**Why this works:** Every dollar of qualifying expense generates 5.75% (Wisconsin) + 18–19% (Federal) = **~24% tax credit**. For a $300K project:
- Revenue to firm: $300K
- Direct costs (team + cloud): $180K (60% cost structure)
- Gross profit: $120K
- **R&D credits (estimated): $43K–$55K** (non-dilutive)
- Net cash impact: **$163K–$175K** from $300K project

## CLOUD COMPUTE & INFRASTRUCTURE COSTS

Computer rental expenses count as qualified research expenses; buying disqualifies them as QREs but qualifies for 100% bonus depreciation starting 2025.

**Strategy: RENT, don't buy.**

**Qualifying cloud costs:**
- AWS EC2 instances (GPU-heavy: p3, p4 instances) for model training
- SageMaker training jobs
- Google Cloud Vertex AI training runs
- Azure ML compute
- Data storage (S3, GCS) for training datasets

**Annual cloud budget (typical project-based):**

- 100% of cloud costs are QREs if used for model development/testing

**Example calculation:**
- Annual cloud spend: $40K
- All training, testing, experimentation (not production inference) = QRE
- Assume 80% of cloud is R&D: $32K qualifying
- State credit: 2.875% × $32K = **$920** (Year 1)

**Spreadsheet tracking (CRITICAL):**

- Aggregate quarterly for credit calculation

## DOCUMENTATION FRAMEWORK (MANDATORY FOR IRS AUDIT DEFENSE)

Your LLC must maintain airtight documentation. Maintain clear records of: Project goals, hypothesis, and technical uncertainty defined at the start; Experiment design, iterations, testing logs, outcomes and next‑step decisions; Time tracking by project, role and research vs non‑research activity; Material/supply usage, cloud compute hours, contractor engagement.

**Project charter (START of every project):**
- Project name, scope, client
- **Technical uncertainty statement:** "Goal: Build ML model to predict X with Y% accuracy. Uncertainty: Unknown optimal architecture (CNN vs. Transformer vs. hybrid), optimal hyperparameters, required training data volume. We must experiment to resolve."
- Hypothesis: "We hypothesize that a hybrid architecture with [spec] will achieve [target]"
- Estimated QREs: Payroll hours, cloud budget, supplier costs

**Weekly/bi-weekly logs (DURING project):**
- Model iterations tested (architecture A, architecture B, etc.)
- Testing results (accuracy before/after, latency, cost)
- Hypothesis validated or rejected
- Next experiment design
- Cloud costs incurred (tag by project)
- Team hours logged by activity type

**Project closeout (END of project):**
- Final model performance
- Summary of experiments conducted (count: 20+ iterations is typical)
- Technical decisions made (why final architecture was chosen)
- Lessons learned / technical breakthroughs

**Quarterly R&D Summary (TAX COMPLIANCE):**
- List all projects active in quarter
- Total payroll hours by project, marked R&D vs. non-R&D
- Cloud costs aggregated by project
- Supplier invoices allocated by project
- Any non-qualifying activities explicitly excluded

**Archive location:**
- Shared drive: `/ProjectDocs/{ProjectName}/RDRecords/`
- Accessible to tax advisor for annual filing

## TAX FILING & REFUND MECHANICS

**Federal (IRC §41):**
- File Form 6765 with your tax return
- Credit type: Incremental or Simplified (use Incremental for better results)
- For small business (<$5M receipts): Credit is **refundable**—you can offset payroll taxes (Form 941) even if no income tax liability
- Startups with <$5M in gross receipts can use the credit to offset up to $500,000 in payroll taxes annually, even if they have no income tax liability.

**Wisconsin (Schedule R):**
- File Wisconsin Schedule R with your state return
- For tax years beginning on or after January 1, 2024, up to 25% of the total R&D tax credit is refundable.
- Excess credit carries forward 15 years (non-refundable portion)

**Example refund timing (Year 1):**
- Calculate R&D credits: $53,865 (federal + state combined)
- Estimate income tax liability Year 1: $20K (you're not yet highly profitable)
- Refundable portion (federal): Remaining $33,865 can offset payroll taxes
- Wisconsin refundable (25%): ~$7,245 × 0.25 = $1,811 cash refund
- **Total Year 1 refund: ~$35K–$40K** (in cash, not just tax offset)

**Refund timing:** IRS processes refund claims within 3–6 months of filing; Wisconsin 2–3 months.

## RISK MITIGATION: IRS AUDIT DEFENSE

**Red flags that trigger R&D credit audits:**
1. No project documentation (no charters, logs, testing records)
2. Too high R&D allocation (claim 95% of payroll is R&D; typical is 60–75%)
3. Mismatch between claimed R&D and actual billable work (claim X hours but client billing shows less)
4. Inadequate technical records (no model comparison results, no iteration logs)

**Your defense (airtight):**
- Weekly timesheets by project and activity type
- Project charters with technical uncertainty statement
- Detailed iteration logs (model A vs. B vs. C results)
- Cloud cost tags showing R&D vs. non-R&D allocation
- Client emails/communications showing technical complexity & challenges
- Performance metrics before/after optimization

**Realistic IRS adjustment:** If audited, IRS might reduce R&D allocation from 70% to 50–60%. Plan conservatively.
